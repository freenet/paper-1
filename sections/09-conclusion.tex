\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Applications on Freenet define their state algebra in sandboxed WebAssembly. A contract-supplied summary/delta protocol synchronizes that state according to application semantics. The underlying small-world ring shapes long-range links through gap-based \textsf{CONNECT} targeting and Kleinberg-style acceptance scoring, while a per-neighbor performance model informs forwarding. Delegates isolate private state and sensitive operations behind a platform-enforced trust boundary.

This architecture targets decentralized group chat, collaborative documents, social feeds, reputation systems, and other applications with mergeable state. Several mechanisms remain experimental or unimplemented. In particular, evaluating asymptotic scaling will require measurements across network sizes rather than further design analysis alone.
